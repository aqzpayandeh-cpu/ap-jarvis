# Jarvis — decisions to collect when the user returns

Ask these one at a time. Do not request passwords, API keys, recovery codes, or broker credentials in chat. Use official OAuth/connection screens and ask for approval at the point an external account is linked.

## Essential launch decisions
1. Do you already own a domain for Jarvis? If yes, what is the domain name?
2. Should the first release be private to you, or publicly accessible with account registration?
3. Which AI provider/model do you prefer, or should we recommend one based on quality, voice, privacy and cost?
4. What monthly running-cost ceiling should Jarvis respect?

## Personal assistant
5. Which Gmail account(s) should be connected?
6. Which university/work Outlook or Microsoft 365 account should be connected?
7. Which calendars should be connected: Apple, Google, Outlook, or more than one?
8. Which actions may Jarvis do without asking (read/search, draft, create reminders), and which always need confirmation (send, invite, delete, reschedule)?
9. What time and days should the daily briefing arrive?

## Voice and memory
10. Do you want natural spoken replies? Any preferred voice or accent?
11. Should voice activation be push-to-talk, a microphone button, or wake-word listening?
12. Should Jarvis remember conversations automatically, or only save details you explicitly ask it to remember?
13. Should memories have categories, expiry rules, and review/export/delete controls?

## Messaging and social
14. Is the WhatsApp use case personal WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business/API?
15. For Instagram, do you want monitoring, drafting, publishing, or all three?
16. Should Jarvis only draft messages/posts, or may it send/publish after a separate confirmation?

## Jobs
17. Confirm target locations, role types, salary floor, working pattern and commute limit.
18. Which job sites should be monitored?
19. May Jarvis tailor CVs and draft applications automatically? Submission will remain approval-gated.
20. Is the CV/profile information currently shown in the dashboard accurate and current?

## Reselling
21. Which marketplaces should be supported first (eBay, Vinted, Depop, Facebook Marketplace, or others)?
22. Is the starting budget still £100, including postage and fees?
23. What minimum net profit and margin make an item worth considering?
24. May Jarvis research and calculate listings automatically while requiring approval before purchases, offers or publishing?

## Trading
25. Confirm instruments and markets: NQ primary and ES context, or anything else?
26. Which charting/broker platforms should be connected (TradingView, MetaTrader, or others)?
27. Is the scope analysis, alerts and journaling only? No automated execution unless separately specified and explicitly approved.
28. Confirm risk limits, funded-account rules and maximum daily loss. The dashboard's 0.5%–2% risk band is only a placeholder, not an instruction to trade.

## Privacy and acceptance
29. Which data may Jarvis store, and what must never be retained?
30. Should sensitive actions require re-authentication or a second confirmation?
31. Who besides you, if anyone, may access this workspace?
32. Do you want an audit history and export/delete controls?
33. What are the three most important things Jarvis must do correctly on day one?
34. What would make you consider the first release complete?

## Approval checkpoints — do not bypass
- Authorize Cloudflare's GitHub connection and select the Pages project in Cloudflare's own UI.
- Authorize each personal account through its provider's official OAuth/consent flow.
- Approve any paid subscription, domain purchase or usage-based service before charges begin.
- Approve any message, job application, purchase, public post, account change or trade before submission/execution.

Never send passwords, API keys, recovery codes or broker credentials in chat. Use provider connection screens or secure secrets settings.
